# Patient Manager Training Guide

## Who This Guide Is For
Patient Managers coordinate care for a specific set of assigned patients. They can only see the patients they have been authorised to manage.

## Your Access
- **Patient Register:** `/PS` — you can only see **patients assigned to you**
- You **cannot** see other patients not in your list

---

## Viewing Your Assigned Patients

1. Go to `/PS` (Patient Register).
2. The list shows **only your assigned patients** — other patients are not visible.
3. Use the search bar to find a specific patient by name, PS number, phone, or CNIC.

> If a patient you are managing does not appear, contact the Administrator to add them to your assignment list.

---

## Patient Profile

Click any patient row to open their profile. You can view:

| Section | What You'll See |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Demographics** | Name, CNIC, gender, age, contact, address, guardian |
| **Visit History** | All past service orders and departments visited |
| **Transactions** | Billing records (read-only) |
| **Treatment Records** | Finalized clinical notes (read-only) |

---

## What You Cannot Do

- You **cannot** register new patients (only receptionists can).
- You **cannot** create or modify transactions.
- You **cannot** edit treatment records.
- You **cannot** see patients not assigned to you.

---

## Getting Patients Assigned to You

Contact the **Administrator** to:
- Add a patient to your management list.
- Remove a patient from your list when they no longer need coordination.
- View the full list of patients currently assigned to you.

---

## Tips
- Use the patient's **PS number** (format: `PS/YYYY/MM/NNNN`) as their unique identifier when coordinating with doctors or reception.
- If a patient has an upcoming appointment, note the department and service order number to track their visit.
- For billing questions, refer the patient to the receptionist or accountant — you have read-only access to transaction records.
