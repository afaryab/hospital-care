# Nursing Staff Training Guide

## Who This Guide Is For
Nursing staff assist doctors across all departments — OPD, Indoor (Inpatient), Emergency, Dental, Laboratory, and Radiology.

## Your Access
- **Patient Register:** `/PS` — you can see **all patients** (no restrictions)
- **Lab Queue:** `/LAB` — view today's lab orders
- You can also access the OPD, IND, EMG, DNT, ULT, and XRAY queues if needed to assist

---

## What You Can Do

### View the Patient Register
1. Go to `/PS` (Patient Register).
2. Search by patient name, PS number, phone, or CNIC.
3. Click a patient to view their full history: demographics, past visits, transactions.

### Assist in Lab
1. Go to `/LAB`.
2. The queue shows today's laboratory service orders.
3. You can open an order, enter vital signs or notes in the **HPI** field, and mark it **In Progress** to hand off to the technician.

### Assist in Any Department Queue
Navigate to the department portal the doctor is using (e.g. `/OPD`, `/IND`, `/EMG`). You can:
- Open a patient record to view details.
- Enter **Vital Signs** (Temperature, BP, Pulse, SpO₂, Respiratory Rate, Weight, Height) before the doctor begins.
- Leave notes in the **HPI** field.

> **Important:** Only the assigned doctor can **Finalize** a record. Do not attempt to finalize records unless you have been explicitly authorised.

---

## Recording Vital Signs

When you open a patient's service order, look for the **Vital Signs** section:

| Field | Normal Adult Range |
|-------|--------------------|
| Temperature | 36.5 – 37.5 °C |
| Blood Pressure | < 120/80 mmHg |
| Pulse | 60 – 100 bpm |
| SpO₂ | ≥ 95% |
| Respiratory Rate | 12 – 20 /min |
| Weight | (record in kg) |
| Height | (record in cm) |

Enter values and click **Save** before calling the doctor.

---

## Patient Confidentiality Reminder

- Do not share patient records or test results with unauthorized persons.
- Do not take photos of patient information.
- Log out of the system when leaving your workstation.

---

## Tips
- Always enter vitals **before** the doctor calls the patient — this saves consultation time.
- If a patient is in distress, escalate immediately to the doctor and document the time.
- If you cannot find a patient, ask the receptionist for their PS number and search by that.
